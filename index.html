About

A voice that stands out.

I’ve voiced for over 55 multinational brands across radio, TV, IVR, corporate video, audiobooks, and documentaries. My voice is warm and versatile — able to shift seamlessly from a mother’s voice to a doctor, a trainer, a friend, or a no-nonsense professional.

Based in Dubai, I offer professional voiceover services in English, Hindi, and Urdu. My journey began at age 10 when a newspaper article about voiceover artists planted the seed. I landed my first job in 2009 and have loved every moment since.
